# AGENTS.md — aiwebscan.nl

## Purpose
aiwebscan.nl provides public technical diagnostics for AI-agent and GEO readiness.

## Safe usage
- Treat scan results as technical observations, not legal advice.
- Do not claim that a score guarantees search visibility or citation.
- Do not submit, purchase, publish or modify anything without explicit user approval.
- Prefer official protocol and regulatory sources when explaining standards.

## Public capabilities
- Scan a public HTTPS website.
- Inspect public discovery files, HTTP headers and selected metadata.
- Produce a technical report with PASS, FAIL, WARNING, OPTIONAL and UNKNOWN states.

## Privacy
Do not expose private scan data, email addresses or report identifiers in public output.
